The Tool

Four things RRK puts on your chart

One overlay, four layers of context. Everything is drawn from price history that has already happened.

Long-Cycle Channel

A 1,400-bar Donchian channel — highest high and lowest low over the longest window most traders never think to plot. It shows where the true extremes sit.

RS / RB Markers

RS (sell-side) and RB (buy-side) print only when price presses a band edge AND closes back inside with a confirmed rejection bar — filtered against trend context so collapses and runaway moves don’t qualify.

OB / OS Momentum Dots

An RSI(14) layer marks momentum extremes at 75/25 crossings, throttled by an 8-bar cooldown so clusters don’t spam your chart.

Context Lines + Alerts

Trend, medium, and slow reference lines frame every marker, and all four events hook straight into TradingView alerts.

By Design

Few signals. On purpose.

Most overlays flood your chart until you can't see price anymore. RRK takes the opposite side: scarcity is the feature.

Read the mechanics in detail

Strict reclaim condition

A marker requires a press into the band plus a close back inside beyond the prior bar’s extreme — one bar that fails the test means no marker.

Cooldown between fires

Once an edge fires, the engine stays quiet for a minimum number of bars. No marker clusters, no noise.

Locked engine

Core lengths, thresholds, and filters are compile-time constants — not exposed knobs. What you see is the same structure everyone else sees.

FAQ

Straight answers

Does RRK predict reversals?

No. It marks reversal-shaped structures after they have completed on closed bars. It is descriptive charting — what you do with a marked structure is entirely your decision.

A band touch alone means nothing — agreed?

Completely. Classic Donchian reading treats band edges as breakout levels, and a touch by itself is noise. RRK waits for the full sequence: press into the edge AND a close back inside beyond the prior bar’s extreme AND trend context still intact. All three or nothing.

Which markets and timeframes?

Any symbol and timeframe available on TradingView, provided the chart has enough loaded history for the 1,400-bar channel. In practice that favors daily+ timeframes and symbols with long histories.

Is it really free right now?

Yes — demo phase. Access is free while we gather feedback. Paid licensing may come later, and early users will always be told before anything changes.

Do I need a paid TradingView plan?

No. Invite-only script access works on free TradingView accounts. Any plan works.

How is access delivered?

TradingView’s invite-only system: we grant your TradingView username access to the protected script. There is nothing to download and nothing to install.

Is this financial advice?

No. Nothing on this site or in the tool is advice, a recommendation, or a solicitation. It is a chart-drawing utility. See our Terms and Risk Disclosure.
