Last updated: August 25, 2026

1. What Reversal King is

Reversal King (“RRK”) is a charting utility: an overlay that draws channel bands, context lines, and markers on price charts inside TradingView. It processes historical price data that has already closed and displays structural information visually.

2. What RRK is not

  • It is not financial advice, a recommendation, or a solicitation to buy or sell anything.
  • It does not predict future prices. Markers describe structures that have already printed on closed bars.
  • It is not an automated trading system and places no trades.
  • It makes no performance claims — no win rates, profit figures, or accuracy statistics are stated or implied anywhere by us.
