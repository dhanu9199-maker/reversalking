This page explains how content on the Reversal King site is produced, reviewed, and kept honest. It applies to every article on this domain.

What we write about

The blog covers one subject in depth: reading band-edge structure and momentum context on price charts — the mechanics of Donchian channels, RSI extremes, moving-average context, and the TradingView workflow around them. We publish within our competence: articles describe tools and bar-by-bar chart mechanics, not markets, economies, or opportunities.

Language rules we hold ourselves to

  1. Tool language only. Indicators show, mark, highlight, or describe structures that have already printed on closed bars. We never write that any tool predicts, forecasts, signals profit, or wins at any rate.
  2. No performance claims. No win rates, accuracy percentages, backtests-as-promises, dollar outcomes, or “proven” framing appear anywhere on this site — not in posts, pages, images, or metadata.
  3. Balanced risk treatment. Every strategy-type article carries a “What this shows, what it doesn’t, and the risks” section. If a piece cannot name its subject’s failure modes honestly, it does not ship.
  4. Past tense for everything verifiable. Structures are described after they exist. Nothing on this site tells you what a market will do next, because nothing can know that — least of all us.

Who writes it

Articles are written by the Reversal King team: the author of the RRK indicator (a self-taught Pine Script v5 developer who built the engine described across this site) working with editorial support that researches, drafts, and fact-checks against primary documentation. See who is behind Reversal King for background and how to reach us.

How drafts become published articles

Every article passes through the same four gates:

  1. Source discipline. Mechanics claims (formula behavior, platform features, indicator parameters) are checked against primary documentation — indicator source code, TradingView’s own docs, original literature such as Wilder 1978. Claims we cannot verify get cut or marked as opinion.
  2. Claims audit. An automated scan checks every page for prohibited claim vocabulary outside explicitly negating contexts (“does not predict”), and a human pass reads for meaning-level violations a grep cannot catch.
  3. Link integrity. Internal links resolve; external links point where they say they point.
  4. Risk-section check. Strategy-adjacent topics ship only with their failure modes named.

Corrections

We fix errors fast and visibly. Factual corrections change the article and bump its review date. If you spot an error, write to reversalking.indicator@gmail.com with the article title and the passage — corrections get priority over everything else in the queue.

Independence

No broker, exchange, prop firm, or data vendor pays us, affiliates us, or previews our content. The site sells nothing in demo phase: there are no sponsored posts, no affiliate links, and no paid placements. The only product discussed is our own indicator, and when we discuss competitors’ tools or general techniques, the standard is the same as anywhere else: describe what it shows, name what it doesn’t.

Not financial advice

Nothing on this site — articles, pages, charts, the indicator itself, or email correspondence — is financial advice, a recommendation, or an offer to trade. We publish descriptions of chart mechanics; you make decisions under your own risk rules. See our full Terms & Risk Disclosure.
