About

One tool. One engine. No magic.

Reversal King started the way most good indicators do: a trader got tired of drawing the same long-horizon channel by hand on every chart, missing the moments when price pressed an extreme and snapped back inside. So it was coded — first for personal use, then refined version after version into what is now RRK X.

The premise is deliberately narrow: plot a very long Donchian channel (1,400 bars), watch the band edges, and mark only high-conviction reclaim structures — a press into the extreme plus a confirmed close back inside, filtered against longer-term trend context. Add RSI-based momentum extremes, keep everything else out.

Why the engine is locked

Most indicator products ship dozens of settings because knobs feel like value. In practice they destroy comparability: your chart and my chart stop showing the same thing. RRK takes the opposite position — the calculation engine is compile-time constants, identical for every user, and only visuals are yours to tune. When you screenshot your chart, everyone reading this site sees exactly the structure you see.

Where this is going

This site is the demo phase: full access, free, in exchange for real feedback from real charts. What happens next depends on what that feedback shows — refinements first, then (if the tool earns it) structured licensing. Paid tiers, when they arrive, will never turn RRK into an advice product. It stays what it is: a lens on price structure.

Operating Principles

How we build

Tool, not oracle

We describe structures that printed. We never promise outcomes, publish win rates, or sell certainty.

Scarcity over spam

Few signals, strictly filtered, identical for all users. If a feature adds noise instead of information, it does not ship.

Feedback-first pricing

Demo phase is free because the product is still learning. Early users shape the roadmap before money enters the picture.

Try the tool while it's free

Demo-phase access costs nothing but honesty: use it on your charts, tell us what holds up.
